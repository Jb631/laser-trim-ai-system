## LOT-BASED DRIFT DETECTION (James's insight, built same evening)
"Isn't the drift detector trying to tell me if a lot is drifting compared to
the previous lots?" — yes, and now it does exactly that.

Empirical design (real-data analysis first, per James's instruction):
* Gap histogram: 41% of consecutive production-day gaps = 1 day; valley at
  4–7d before the changeover mass → LOT = day-cluster, new lot after >3 idle
  days. 3,683 lots, median 14 units/lot, 105 models with ≥8 lots.
* Premise check: between-lot σ of lot medians vs within-lot unit σ — only
  resistance shows the classic huge lot effect (6607: 7.9×); most metrics
  wander modestly. Conclusion: calibrate σ on the OBSERVED spread of lot
  medians — normal lot wander becomes the noise floor.

Implementation: ml/lots.py (clustering, open-lot rule, per-metric loader);
trainer observes LOT MEDIANS (robust to corrupt units by construction),
baseline = all-but-newest-3 closed lots (min 8 lots, tiny lots n<3 excluded
from baseline σ), σ floored at 1% of |mean|; replay of newest closed lots;
advance ingests only newly-CLOSED lots (date watermark; last_row_id=None
marks lot-mode rows; legacy rows keep the old path until retrained).
compute_recent_means → last closed lot median (all surfaces consistent).
Language: cards "last lot vs baseline", drift tab "Baseline (lot mean±σ) /
Last lot / Lot size", verdict "last lot +Xσ vs baseline lots", σ glosses.

RESULT on the real DB (279 models retrained in one pass — lots are cheap):
* BEFORE (unit mode): 97 flagged (56 OOC / 20 drift / 21 warning)
* AFTER (lot mode):   26 flagged (13 OOC / 6 drift / 7 warning); 9 in
  Active scope — a reviewable morning list.
* The two extreme flags are REAL: 8711-S lot medians 850Ω→4,900Ω→9,922Ω
  across builds (an unrequalified design change — the button's exact use
  case) and 7122's final two lots degrading 86× before production stopped.
Tests: one-alarm-per-lot (60-unit shifted lot = one bounded CUSUM push),
open-lot exclusion + ingest-once, requalify floors lot history, preset
consistency; drift test family migrated to weekly-lot seeds. Sweeps 53/0.
